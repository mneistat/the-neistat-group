Pre-market exposure

Top Agent Network — Exclusive Pre-Market Access

Before your home ever hits the MLS, it goes to the Top Agent Network — an invitation-only private listing network of the top 10% of brokers in Illinois, ranked by production. These are the agents with the most active, qualified buyers. A pre-market sale means no days on market, no open houses, and often a premium price.

What this means: Your home may sell before anyone else even knows it's available.

Jameson Network & Broker Relationships

Jameson Sotheby's has over 400 agents across Chicago and the North Shore — all of whom receive direct outreach before your listing launches publicly. Combined with Matthew's personal relationships with top producing brokers across the city, your home gets in front of the right people before anyone else.

What this means: 400+ agents know about your home before it hits Zillow.

Public launch

MLS Launch & Digital Syndication

Strategic MLS timing — Thursday or Friday launch to maximize first-weekend traffic. Instant syndication to the Sotheby's International Realty website, Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, Homes.com, and 100+ real estate portals nationwide. Every buyer searching in your price range and neighborhood sees your listing.

What this means: Maximum eyeballs in the first 72 hours — when it matters most.

Instagram & Targeted Social Advertising

Paid advertising on Instagram and Facebook targeted by location, income, age, and buying behavior. We reach buyers who are actively looking in your neighborhood and price range — not just people who happen to scroll past.

What this means: Your listing finds buyers — not the other way around.
