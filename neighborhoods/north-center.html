Orientation

North Center and Northcenter are not quite the same thing.

North Center is one of Chicago’s official community areas. Northcenter is the smaller neighborhood identity and business district centered around Lincoln Avenue and the Town Square. Roscoe Village sits within the broader community area but has its own guide here.

Housing

What you can buy here.

Housing varies from older two- and three-flats and frame homes to renovated single-family houses, condominiums, and newer infill. The exact mix changes between the commercial corridors, river edge, and interior residential streets.

Two- and three-flats

Vintage brick and frame multi-unit buildings remain a core part of the neighborhood.

Single-family homes

Older homes, substantial renovations, and newer rebuilds appear throughout the residential streets.

Condos

Condominiums range from vintage conversions to boutique elevator buildings and mixed-use corridors.

New construction

Infill development continues alongside established housing stock rather than in one concentrated district.
