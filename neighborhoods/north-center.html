Boundaries

North Center and Northcenter are not quite the same thing.

North Center is one of Chicago’s official community areas. Northcenter is the smaller neighborhood identity and business district centered around Lincoln Avenue and the Town Square. Roscoe Village sits within the broader community area but has its own guide here.
