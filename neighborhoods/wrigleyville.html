Orientation

Wrigleyville is a district within Lakeview.

The name is commonly used for the area surrounding Wrigley Field rather than a separate official Chicago community area. For a property decision, the useful question is how close the address is to the ballpark, Clark Street, transit, Southport, and quieter residential blocks.

Clark & Addison

Wrigley Field and the highest concentration of game-day activity sit at the center of the district.

Housing

What you can buy here.

The housing mix is closely related to Lakeview as a whole, but proximity to Wrigley Field, Clark Street, transit, and quieter side streets can materially change the experience of an address.

Two- and three-flats

Vintage brick and greystone multi-unit buildings remain common on residential streets.

Condos

Units range from vintage conversions to newer boutique buildings and larger developments.

Single-family homes

Renovated older homes and newer rebuilds appear on side streets throughout the area.

New construction

Infill projects continue to add newer housing among the neighborhood’s older building stock.
