Housing

What you can buy here.

The housing mix ranges from vintage flats, greystones, and courtyard buildings to condos, frame houses, and newer infill. Building type and location can change quickly within the neighborhood.

Two- and three-flats

Vintage brick and greystone multi-unit buildings remain a major part of Lakeview’s residential fabric.

Condos & courtyard buildings

Condominiums range from vintage conversions to larger courtyard and elevator buildings.

Frame houses & single-family

Older frame homes and renovated single-family properties appear throughout the neighborhood.

New construction

Small infill condominium and single-family projects continue to appear on individual sites.
