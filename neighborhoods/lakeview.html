Neighborhood Intelligence

Lakeview

Three-flats, courtyard buildings, lakefront parkland, and Wrigley Field on Chicago’s North Side.

The Essence

Several neighborhoods under one name.

Lakeview is the everyday name for the Lake View community area, and it holds several distinct places: Wrigleyville around the ballpark, Northalsted along Halsted Street, and the blocks along the lake. Much of its housing was built between the 1880s and the 1920s — frame houses, greystones, and brick flats — while in East Lake View, apartment buildings from the late 1940s through the 1960s replaced smaller houses and flats or filled vacant lots.

The Map

Diversey to Irving Park, Ravenswood to the lake — and north to Montrose west of Clark.

Boundaries approximate.

At a Glance

Housing Stock

Two- and three-flats, greystones, frame houses, courtyard buildings, and tall apartment buildings near the lake.

Transit & Access

CTA Red, Brown, and Purple Line Express stations, bus routes, and DuSable Lake Shore Drive ramps at Belmont and Irving Park.

Parks

Lincoln Park’s lakefront, Belmont Harbor, the Marovitz golf course, and neighborhood parks such as Gill Park.

Landmark Districts

Six Chicago Landmark districts, including Alta Vista Terrace, Newport Avenue, and Surf-Pine Grove.

Housing Stock

Two- and Three-Flats

Brick and greystone flat buildings with one apartment per floor above a raised basement, many from the 1890s onward.

Greystones

Buildings with front facades clad in gray limestone; Pine Grove Avenue has a landmark row of eight from 1891.

Frame Houses

Wood-frame houses from before annexation, when Lake View was outside Chicago’s post-Fire ban on frame construction.

Courtyard Buildings

Corner and courtyard apartment buildings, alongside tall apartment buildings and apartment hotels near the lake.

New Construction

Small infill condominium buildings on individual lots, sometimes replacing older buildings.

Open Space

Parks & Open Space

Lake View’s entire shoreline is Lincoln Park parkland, with Belmont Harbor, the Sydney R. Marovitz Golf Course, the Waveland tennis courts, and the Diversey Driving Range along it. Inland, the neighborhood parks are small — most under an acre — and Gill Park, on Sheridan Road, has a fieldhouse with an indoor pool and gymnasium.
