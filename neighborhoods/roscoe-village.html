Boundaries

A neighborhood with defined boundaries.

Roscoe Village Neighbors defines the neighborhood as Addison Street on the north, Belmont Avenue on the south, Ravenswood Avenue on the east, and the Chicago River on the west.

The neighborhood’s principal pedestrian-oriented commercial street runs through the residential fabric.

What Matters Here

Parks, schools, and everyday life.

Roscoe Village has a clearly defined neighborhood footprint, but daily life still changes block by block between Roscoe Street, the river, the residential interior, and the larger corridors on its edges.
