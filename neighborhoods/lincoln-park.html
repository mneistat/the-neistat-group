Lincoln Park

South Pond, neighborhood parks, schools, and local places from the lakefront to the residential streets.

The Essence

Start with what surrounds you.

The Nature Boardwalk and Lily Pool bring different landscapes to the lakefront park. Oz and Trebes parks provide places to play farther into the neighborhood. Between them are schools, the zoo, the Conservatory, and a seasonal farmers market — useful places to know before considering a particular address.

Parks & Open Space

From South Pond to the neighborhood interior.

Nature Boardwalk

South Pond at Lincoln Park Zoo

Native planting around South Pond brings prairie, wetland, and savanna habitats into the park. The boardwalk is also a research site for the zoo’s Urban Wildlife Institute.

Explore the boardwalk →

Oz Park

2021 N. Burling Street

Tennis courts, ball fields, basketball, a community garden, and Dorothy’s Playground sit among sculptures of the Oz characters.

Park details & programs →

Alfred Caldwell Lily Pool

125 W. Fullerton Parkway

Limestone paths, a pavilion, and native planting frame Caldwell’s 1930s landscape. Check seasonal access before planning a visit.

Trebes Park

2250 N. Clifton Avenue

At a Glance

Transit & Access

  • Fullerton — Red, Brown & Purple Line Express
  • Armitage — Brown & Purple Line Express
  • Purple Line Express runs during weekday rush periods. Station pages include bus connections and accessibility notices.

Armitage & Halsted

Above the storefronts, pressed-metal bays, cornices, and corner turrets distinguish the Armitage-Halsted landmark district. Its streets mix small residences with taller mixed-use buildings.

Explore the landmark district →

Housing Stock

Greystones

Limestone-fronted rowhouses from the turn of the last century, often with original interior detail.

Single-Family Homes

Restored vintage houses and full gut rehabs, concentrated on interior residential streets.

2–4 Flats

Small multi-unit brick buildings, bought both as owner-occupied homes and as long-term holds.

Condos

Conversions in vintage buildings alongside units in newer boutique elevator buildings.

New Construction

Infill single-family homes and small-scale developments, typically on individual lots.

Market Notes

Calendar year 2025 · Lincoln Park community area

The housing type changes the comparison.

Condominiums accounted for most recorded residential sales in Lincoln Park in 2025. Single-family properties and entire apartment buildings made up smaller, distinct parts of the market. A useful comparison starts with the same property type, then narrows to the block, condition, parking, and outdoor space.

Matt’s Broker Note

In Lincoln Park, the block matters as much as the neighborhood. Outdoor space, parking, school boundaries, and building quality can change the buyer pool fast.
