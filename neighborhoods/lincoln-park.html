Neighborhood Intelligence

Lincoln Park

Historic homes, lakefront access, parks, and block-by-block pricing dynamics on Chicago’s North Side.

The Essence

A neighborhood of contrast and character.

Lincoln Park is not one market. East Lincoln Park, DePaul, Ranch Triangle, and the western edges can each behave differently in pricing, inventory, and buyer demand. It is a neighborhood where historic architecture, green space, and urban convenience come together — and where the right block can make all the difference.

Housing Stock

Greystones

Limestone-fronted rowhouses from the turn of the last century, often with original interior detail.

Single-Family Homes

Restored vintage houses and full gut rehabs, concentrated on interior residential streets.

2–4 Flats

Small multi-unit brick buildings, bought both as owner-occupied homes and as long-term holds.

Condos

Conversions in vintage buildings alongside units in newer boutique elevator buildings.

New Construction

Infill single-family homes and small-scale developments, typically on individual lots.
