03 / THE PROCESS

One decision at a time.

Begin with the valuation. Choose a sale process only after reviewing the evidence.

  1. 01

    Gather the records

    Share available expenses, rents, capital improvements, and building details.

  2. 02

    Review the valuation

    Compare the offer with a supported range, assumptions, and capital needs.

  3. 03

    Choose a path

    Agree on scope, representation, compensation, and outreach if the board wants to proceed.

  4. 04

    Evaluate any offers

    Review price and terms, with counsel guiding required approvals and any sale.

Timing depends on record availability, the selected strategy, buyer diligence, and the approval process. A valuation does not commit the association to a sale.

FOR THE BOARD

The finer details.

Expand a topic for background and sources.

Approval, ownership percentages & owner rights+

Chicago requires at least 85% approval for a full-building sale unless the declaration or bylaws require more. Counsel must confirm voting interests, procedures, and owner protections.

Resolve the 66-parcel / 67-unit discrepancy before calculating votes or allocating proceeds. A qualifying sale can bind dissenting owners; counsel should explain objection deadlines and applicable appraisal, debt, and relocation protections.

Read Chicago Municipal Code §13-72-085 ↗
Condominium financing changes+

Fannie Mae’s 2026 changes affect loans subject to its standards. A lender must assess this building’s eligibility.

  • March 18, 2026: The 50% investment-property concentration limit was removed for established projects using Full Review on investor loans.
  • July 1, 2026: Covered loan applications must meet a $50,000 maximum per-unit master-policy deductible for required perils.
  • August 3, 2026: For applications from this date, Limited Review is retired. Reserve-study flexibility requires the highest recommended allocation; baseline funding is not accepted.
  • January 4, 2027: Full Review applications face a standard 15% replacement-reserve allocation, up from 10%. A qualifying reserve study may support an alternative.
Fannie Mae lender letter LL-2026-03 ↗

Source reviewed September 21, 2026. These are Fannie Mae loan standards, not a determination of this building’s eligibility. Confirm the applicable current requirements with the lender.
