The Buying Process

Eight steps from first budget conversation to keys in hand. Knowing the shape of it in advance is what keeps the process calm.

Eight Steps

From budget to closing.

Every purchase follows the same arc. What changes is how well prepared you are at each stage.

01

Understand your budget

What you can borrow and what you want to spend are two different numbers. Start with the second one.

02

Get pre-approved

A pre-approval letter turns you from a browser into a buyer. Sellers treat the two very differently.

03

Define neighborhoods and property type

Narrow the search before you tour. Knowing what you are looking for is what makes it possible to recognise it.

04

Tour strategically

See enough to calibrate, not so much that everything blurs together. Every showing should answer a question.

05

Offer strategy

Price is one term among several. Timing, contingencies and certainty of close often matter as much.

06

Attorney review and inspection

The contract becomes real here. Findings get negotiated, and the deal takes its final shape.

07

Financing and appraisal

The lender confirms what you and the seller already agreed. Surprises at this stage are manageable if anticipated.

08

Closing

Final walkthrough, documents, funding, keys.

Start with the numbers.

Work out what a purchase actually costs each month, then let's talk about the property and the strategy.
