For Buyers / Buying Process

Your next home, with a plan.

Start with how you want to live, what you’re comfortable spending, and the timing of your move. I help you compare the options and understand each decision along the way.

Eight Steps

From first conversation to keys in hand.

These are the main decisions we work through together. Some stages overlap; the property, your financing, and the contract shape the timing.

01

Define the move

We start with your priorities, preferred neighborhoods, and what needs to happen before you can move. Before touring together, we agree on services, responsibilities, and negotiable compensation in a written buyer agreement.

02

Set your budget and financing

Consider the monthly cost, including taxes, insurance, and any association fees, plus cash needed for closing. If you’re financing, work with your lender on loan options and pre-approval. Pre-approval is conditional; it does not guarantee final loan approval.

03

Search public and private opportunities

Alongside public listings, we can explore Jameson exclusives, Top Agent Network, and MRED’s Private Listing Network where available and authorized. Broker conversations can help surface possibilities for your next home. Availability and sharing permissions vary.

04

Tour with a purpose

Compare layout, condition, building costs, and the day-to-day setting. Each visit helps us refine the search, identify questions for the right specialists, and decide whether a property deserves a closer look.

05

Build the offer around your priorities

Review comparable sales and choose the price and terms you’re comfortable offering. We weigh closing timing, financing, earnest money, and contingencies together, including any need to sell your current home. The seller may accept, counter, or decline.

06

Review the contract and the property

Your attorney reviews the agreement and relevant property documents; your inspector evaluates the home’s condition. We work through findings and any requests to the seller. Your contract determines the review periods, deadlines, and available options.

07

Complete financing and appraisal

If you’re financing, your lender completes its review and arranges any required valuation. An appraisal estimates the home’s value, which may differ from the purchase price. We work through any issues with your lender and attorney.

08

Prepare to close and move

Review final figures and documents with your lender and attorney, complete the walkthrough, and confirm funds, possession, and moving arrangements. I help keep the remaining steps organized.

Buying and Selling

One plan for both moves.

If you own a home already, the purchase and sale need to work together. We plan around your cash, timing, and where you’ll live during the transition.

Decide what needs to happen first

Identify whether the purchase depends on sale proceeds, what your lender can support, and how much overlap you can manage. We can explore private opportunities while preparing your current property, then choose a sequence that fits your situation.

Work through timing and tradeoffs

Discuss closing dates, possession, and whether a home-sale contingency belongs in your offer. Any requested terms need the other party’s agreement. We also consider what happens if one transaction moves faster than the other.

Let’s plan your next move.

Tell me what you’re looking for, your timing, and whether you have a property to sell. We’ll work through the next decision together.
